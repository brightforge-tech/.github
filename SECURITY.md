# Security policy

This policy applies to every BrightForge Technologies repository that does not
have its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security problems in public issues, discussions or
pull requests.**

1. Preferred: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose
   **Report a vulnerability** (GitHub private reporting).
2. Or email [info@brightforgett.com](mailto:info@brightforgett.com) with the
   subject `SECURITY: <project name>`.

Please include what the problem is, what an attacker could do with it, steps
to reproduce, and the version or commit affected.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge reports within **3 business days**.
- We aim to confirm the issue and share a fix plan within **14 days**.
- We credit reporters in release notes unless you prefer otherwise.
- Please allow up to **90 days** for a fix before public disclosure.
