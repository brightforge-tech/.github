# Contributing to BrightForge Labs projects

Thanks for helping! This guide applies to every BrightForge repository that
does not have its own `CONTRIBUTING.md` — check the project's README first.

- **Questions and ideas:** use the project's Discussions tab.
- **Bugs:** open an issue with steps to reproduce and the version you use.
- **Security problems:** never in public — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Pull requests:** keep them focused, include tests, and describe what
  changed and why. Never commit credentials or personal data.

## Contributor Licence Agreement

BrightForge Labs projects are open source (usually AGPL-3.0) and also offered
under commercial terms, which funds their development. Before your first pull
request is merged, a bot will ask you to agree to the project's Contributor
Licence Agreement with a one-line comment. You keep the copyright in your
work.

## Code of conduct

Everyone is expected to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
